How do I enable Pretty Jason in Safari on Mac?

Open the Pretty Jason app and click Open Safari Extensions Settings…, then tick Pretty Jason and allow it on the websites you use.

How do I enable it on iPhone or iPad?

Open Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions › Pretty Jason, turn Allow Extension on, and set websites to Allow.

What shortcuts are built in?

  • Press / to filter keys and values. Escape clears the filter and restores your fold state.
  • Option-click a disclosure arrow (▼) to fold or unfold its entire branch.
  • Click any key to copy its JavaScript path (user.name), or Option-click a key to copy its value.

Where do I report a bug or request a feature?

Open an issue on GitHub or email company@lineofflig.ht.
