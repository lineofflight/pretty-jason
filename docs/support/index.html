Support

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub.

Enable on Mac

Open the Pretty Jason app, click Open Safari Extensions Settings…, tick Pretty Jason, and allow it on the websites you use.

Enable on iPhone and iPad

Open Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions › Pretty Jason, turn on Allow Extension, and allow it on websites.

Shortcuts

  • Press / to filter keys and values. Escape clears the filter.
  • Option-click a disclosure arrow (▼) to fold or unfold its whole branch.
  • Click a key to copy its path (user.name). Option-click it to copy its value.
