# Privacy Policy

Pretty Jason runs entirely in your browser.

- No personal data or browsing history is collected, stored, or transmitted.
- Parsing and formatting happen locally on your device.
- Page access is used only to detect and format JSON documents.
- The Headers tab re-requests the current page from its own server, only when you open that tab.
- Nothing is shared with third parties.
